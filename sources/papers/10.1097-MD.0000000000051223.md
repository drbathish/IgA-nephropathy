Study summary (for evidence-reviewer, not a substitute for full extraction): Single-arm, single-institution retrospective study of 12 treatment-naïve primary IgAN patients given 6 months of combined Nefecon (16 mg/day) + irbesartan (150 mg/day, fixed dose). Estimated mean 24h urinary protein reductions from baseline: -28.28% (1 month), -32.78% (3 months), -51.46% (6 months), all p<.001. eGFR stable at 1 month, rose significantly at 3 and 6 months (p=.009). Gd-IgA1 change at 1 month correlated with urinary protein change (r=0.664, p=.018). Urinary dysmorphic RBCs decreased markedly by 6 months. Authors explicitly caveat: given the single-arm design, changes cannot be attributed to the combination itself (could reflect either agent alone, natural fluctuation, or regression to the mean); subgroup findings (e.g., sex-based difference) are exploratory/hypothesis-generating; confirmation in adequately powered controlled studies required. Small sample, low evidentiary weight, self-acknowledged by authors — flag for evidence-reviewer's quality appraisal.
