Study summary (for evidence-reviewer, not a substitute for full extraction): Investigates nephritis-associated plasmin receptor (NAPlr), a streptococcal GAPDH previously identified as nephritogenic in post-streptococcal GN, as a candidate mechanism in IgAV. Skin biopsies from IgAV patients showed NAPlr/plasmin-activity staining in 7/11 patients vs. minimal staining in controls; serum anti-NAPlr IgG/IgA were significantly elevated in IgAV (n=21) vs. healthy donors (n=18); anti-NAPlr IgG positively correlated with serum creatinine and negatively with CH50; serum Gd-IgA1 was elevated and positively correlated with anti-NAPlr IgA but negatively with C4. Suggests an infection-triggered (streptococcal) immune mechanism contributing to IgAV via complement activation — relevant to the mucosal-immunity/infectious-trigger pathogenesis sub-topic (complements the already-recorded Filippatos et al. 2026 infectious-triggers review, Batch 6).
