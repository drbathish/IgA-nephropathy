- Notes (literature-scout, not a screening decision): Elderly woman developed biopsy-confirmed IgA vasculitis (skin + renal involvement, fibrotic crescent formation on follow-up renal biopsy) during trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1) therapy for HER2-positive breast cancer; treated with prednisolone and cyclophosphamide with clinical remission. Authors frame this as a rare but clinically important adverse-drug-reaction recognition point (anti-HER2 antibody-drug conjugate triggering secondary IgAV) rather than a treatment-efficacy study of IgAV itself. This is a novel disease-association/causality case rather than a novel therapeutic intervention for IgAV — flagged for evidence-reviewer to judge whether this fits the tier-7 "novel/emerging... diagnostic pitfall" carve-out (recognizing drug-induced IgAV) or should be excluded as a single-patient case report without a qualifying carve-out.
