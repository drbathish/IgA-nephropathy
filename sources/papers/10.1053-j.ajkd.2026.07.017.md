## Summary (for evidence-reviewer; not a screening decision)

MAJOR FINDING — this is the first RCT-level evidence found in this project's search history directly testing rituximab in IgAN (prior batches, e.g. Batch 2 Run 14, found no rituximab RCT for IgAN; only off-topic nephrotic-syndrome rituximab trials and an uncontrolled case series were identified). The RITA trial: multicenter (10 Chinese centers), n=106 adults with biopsy-proven IgAN, UTP >1 g/day despite ≥3 months RASI therapy, randomized to RTX+RASI vs. RASI alone for 12 months. Primary endpoint (24-hour UTP change at month 12) showed NO significant difference between arms (adjusted GMR 1.05, 95% CI 0.80-1.36). No significant differences in eGFR, serum Gd-IgA1, or proportion achieving ≥50% proteinuria reduction. Authors' own conclusion: "No additional reduction in proteinuria or preservation of kidney function was observed with the addition of RTX to optimized RASI-based therapy." Self-acknowledged limitations: short follow-up, Asian-only population. This is a NEGATIVE/NULL tier-2 RCT — an important addition to the evidence base (fills the previously flagged "no rituximab RCT for IgAN" gap identified in Batch 2) and should be weighed alongside the still-unresolved, conference-abstract-only Trivioli et al. rituximab-in-IgAV dataset (distinct disease/population, not a substitute for it).
