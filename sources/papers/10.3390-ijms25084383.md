Classified as tier 5 (cross-sectional/case-control comparison predominates: baseline biomarker levels compared between IgAV patients who did/did not develop nephritis and healthy controls) rather than tier 3, since the reported analysis is fundamentally a case-control biomarker comparison rather than a fully powered prospective cohort study with prespecified longitudinal endpoints; the 6-month follow-up component supports the outcome-association claim but does not by itself elevate the design to tier 3. Single-country (Croatia), pediatric-only cohort flagged as a generalizability limitation.
- Date added: 2026-09-13
- Study type: prospective case-control study; n=86 pediatric IgAV patients, 70 controls, with 6-month follow-up
- Notes: Tier-6 (or arguably tier-5 cross-sectional/prospective) mechanistic-biomarker evidence directly on IgAV (not extrapolated from IgAN) — fills the IgAV mechanism/biomarker sub-topic gap. Key finding: urinary HMGB1 was higher in IgAV patients who developed nephritis vs those without, and correlated with albuminuria and hematuria — a candidate biomarker for IgAV nephritis risk/monitoring. Single-country (Croatia) pediatric cohort — generalizability to adults not established.
