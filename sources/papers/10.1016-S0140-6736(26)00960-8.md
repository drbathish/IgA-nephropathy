- Notes: Tier-2 RCT evidence, and notably placebo-controlled (unlike the active-comparator PROTECT sparsentan trials) — directly addresses the stated priority for controlled/comparative IgAN evidence. Key secondary endpoint (eGFR change at week 136) showed a between-group difference of 2.4 mL/min/1.73m2 that did not reach the prespecified significance threshold (95% CI -0.1 to 4.8; p=0.057), though the total eGFR slope difference (1.4 mL/min/1.73m2/year, 95% CI 0.5-2.3) favored atrasentan. In the SGLT2-inhibitor stratum, the eGFR difference was larger (9.1 mL/min/1.73m2, 95% CI 3.0-15.2). Funded by Novartis (manufacturer) — sponsor-funded industry trial, note for bias appraisal.
