- Study type: retrospective multicenter cohort study; n=1148 children with biopsy-proven IgAVN, 41 international pediatric nephrology centres, 25 countries, 2005-2019, median follow-up 3.7 years
- Notes: Large-scale tier-4/5 real-world evidence (largest IgAVN cohort found this session), directly relevant to the treatment/therapeutics sub-topic. Key finding: no specific second-line immunosuppressive regimen was superior to others, even in higher-risk subgroups — an important negative/null finding and evidence gap that should be represented in synthesis (per research-question.md's requirement to represent evidence gaps, not just positive findings). Predictors of poor outcome: older age, lower eGFR at onset, hypertension, tubular atrophy/segmental sclerosis on histology. Retrospective design (no randomization) is a limitation for treatment-comparison claims specifically, even though the cohort itself is large and well-described for prognostic purposes.
