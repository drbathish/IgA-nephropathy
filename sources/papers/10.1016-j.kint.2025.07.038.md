High-resolution confocal microscopy study of glomerular immune-complex deposits (C3, IgA, IgG) in kidney biopsies from patients with IgAN vs. IgAVN. Key findings, reported SEPARATELY for each disease per research-question.md's topic-specific constraint: in IgAN, C3-IgA colocalization correlated with active histologic injury (mesangial hypercellularity, endocapillary hypercellularity, crescents) and with higher MEST-C (M+E+C) composite scores; in IgAVN, C3-IgA and IgA-IgG colocalization were similarly elevated but the correlation with individual active-injury features was not significant (only the composite score trend reached significance for C3-IgA and, uniquely in IgAVN, for C3-IgG). Overall supports a nephritogenic role for C3-IgA-containing immune complexes in both diseases while highlighting a disease-specific difference in IgG/complement involvement (more peripheral capillary deposits and higher C3-IgG/IgA-IgG colocalization in IgAVN than IgAN).
