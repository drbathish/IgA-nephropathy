Study summary (for evidence-reviewer, not a substitute for full extraction): Lausanne cohort (n=63 IgAN kidney-transplant recipients, 2003-2019) found NO association between glomerular crescents in native kidney biopsies and IgAN recurrence in the renal allograft. Meta-analysis pooling 5 additional cohorts (total n=311, 23% with recurrent IgAN; crescent prevalence 12-62% across cohorts) confirmed the absence of association (RR 1.53, 95% CI 0.40-5.90, p=.41). Authors note crescents (particularly >25% of viable glomeruli) remain an unfavorable prognostic factor for progression to kidney failure in native IgAN, but do NOT appear to predict recurrence risk after transplantation — a notable null/negative finding relevant to transplant-recurrence risk stratification, complementing the already-recorded Rath et al. 2026 J Nephrology systematic review (Batch 8) on Oxford MEST-C component prognostic value in transplant/recurrent IgAN.
