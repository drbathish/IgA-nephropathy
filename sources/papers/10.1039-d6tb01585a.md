## Summary (for evidence-reviewer; not a screening decision)

Preclinical mechanistic study: M2 macrophage-derived extracellular vesicles (EVs) isolated from polarized peritoneal macrophages, tested in a murine IgAN model plus LPS-injured podocytes in vitro. Three weeks of EV treatment improved survival/body weight, reduced proteinuria/UACR/serum creatinine/BUN, and attenuated mesangial expansion, podocyte effacement, fibrosis, and mesangial IgA deposition. Proteomic profiling showed a shift toward redox-balance pathway enrichment and reduced fibrosis/inflammation pathways; EVs reduced renal mitochondrial ROS, increased SOD2, restored PI3K/AKT phosphorylation in podocytes, decreased M1/M2 macrophage ratio, increased Treg accumulation. Entirely preclinical (murine + in vitro); no human data. Represents a novel candidate nanotherapeutic mechanism (immune-oxidative microenvironment reprogramming), distinct from the named pharmacologic/biologic therapies in research-question.md's scope.
