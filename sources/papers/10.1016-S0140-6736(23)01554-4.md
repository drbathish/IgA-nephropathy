- Notes: TIER-2 RCT EVIDENCE — a second major placebo-controlled phase 3 trial (alongside ALIGN/atrasentan) directly filling the previously identified evidence gap. Primary endpoint (time-weighted average eGFR over 2 years) showed a highly significant treatment benefit (difference 5.05 mL/min/1.73m2, 95% CI 3.24-7.38, p<0.0001). Notable adverse event imbalance (peripheral oedema 17% vs 4%, hypertension 12% vs 3%, muscle spasms 12% vs 4%, acne 11% vs 1% — Nefecon vs placebo) — relevant to safety/tolerability synthesis. Funded by Calliditas Therapeutics (manufacturer) — sponsor-funded industry trial, note for bias appraisal.
