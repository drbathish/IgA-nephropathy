## Summary (for evidence-reviewer; not a screening decision)

Human mechanistic study (tonsil tissue, renal tissue, peripheral blood, in vitro co-culture) examining CXCR3+ T cells in IgAN. Found greater CXCR3+ cell accumulation in interfollicular areas of IgAN tonsils vs. non-IgAN tonsils; CXCR3+ cells and IP-10 detected in renal tubulointerstitium; higher chemotactic response of tonsillar mononuclear cells to IP-10 in IgAN; higher proportion of CXCR3+CD3+ cells (Th1/Tc1-associated phenotype) in IgAN tonsils; co-culture showed CXCR3+ T cells enhanced Gd-IgA1 production by tonsillar B cells. Tonsillar CXCR3+CD3+ proportions were higher in patients achieving clinical remission after tonsillectomy; circulating CXCR3+CD3+ cells decreased post-tonsillectomy in IgAN but not controls. Supports the tonsillar-focal-disease / mucosal-immunity mechanistic model via a CXCR3-IP-10 axis. No sample size reported in abstract — flagged for evidence-reviewer to check methods section for n.
