Note (content summary, for evidence-reviewer, not an extraction): Multicenter retrospective cohort (n=788 biopsy-proven adult IgAN, diagnosed 2002-2021, median follow-up 70 months). Hb <11.0 g/dL independently associated with higher ESKD risk vs. reference (12.0-12.9 g/dL) band (aHR 2.88, 95% CI 1.24-6.68, p=0.014); association significant in women (aHR 5.10 for Hb<11.0; aHR 4.66 for Hb 11.0-11.9) but not significant in men on sex-stratified analysis. Lower Hb also associated with more severe tubular atrophy/interstitial fibrosis and endocapillary hypercellularity on biopsy. Authors propose Hb as a simple prognostic/risk-stratification marker, with a sex-specific effect that is itself a notable finding for research-question.md's biomarker sub-topic.
