Study summary (for evidence-reviewer, not a substitute for full extraction): Prespecified interim analysis of an ongoing double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial of ravulizumab (IV, every 8 weeks) in adults with IgAN (eGFR ≥30 mL/min/1.73m², proteinuria ≥0.75 UPCR or ≥1.0 g/day) on stable standard therapy. 485 randomized 1:1 (240 ravulizumab, 245 placebo); interim analysis population n=352 (176 per arm). Primary endpoint: 24h UPCR change from baseline at week 34 — ravulizumab reduced UPCR by 46.6% vs. 5.6% with placebo (adjusted geometric-mean difference 43.4% lower with ravulizumab, 99% CI 30.1–54.2, p<0.001). Adverse events: 69.5% (ravulizumab) vs 75.8% (placebo); serious AEs 4.6% vs 9.0%; no meningococcal infections or deaths. Funded by Alexion/AstraZeneca Rare Disease Unit — sponsor-funded industry trial, flag for bias appraisal. Interim/surrogate (proteinuria) endpoint only at this stage, not a hard clinical outcome (eGFR slope/kidney failure) — flag for evidence-reviewer re: strength-of-evidence tier relative to the final-analysis RCTs already on file (e.g., APPLAUSE-IgAN final, ALIGN).
