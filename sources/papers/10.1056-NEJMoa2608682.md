Study summary (for evidence-reviewer, not a substitute for full extraction): Prespecified interim analysis of an ongoing double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial of povetacicept (80 mg subcutaneous every 4 weeks) vs. placebo (2:1 randomization) in biopsy-proven IgAN patients on stable background therapy. 557 received study drug (372 povetacicept, 185 placebo); efficacy analysis population n=199 (131 povetacicept, 68 placebo). Primary endpoint: 24h UPCR change at week 36 — povetacicept reduced UPCR by 52.0% vs. 4.3% with placebo (adjusted difference −49.8 percentage points, 95% CI −58.6 to −39.2, p<0.001). Secondary endpoints: Gd-IgA1 decreased 77.4% (povetacicept) vs. increased 9.1% (placebo) (adjusted difference −79.3 points, 95% CI −82.2 to −75.9, p<0.001); hematuria resolution in 85% (povetacicept) vs. 23% (placebo) among those with baseline hematuria (adjusted difference 62 points, 95% CI 45–78, p<0.001). AE rates similar between arms (75% vs 78%); serious AEs 3% vs 4%. Funded by Vertex Pharmaceuticals — sponsor-funded industry trial, flag for bias appraisal. Interim/surrogate (proteinuria, Gd-IgA1, hematuria) endpoints only at this stage, not a hard clinical outcome (eGFR slope/kidney failure) — flag for evidence-reviewer re: strength-of-evidence tier relative to final-analysis RCTs already on file.
