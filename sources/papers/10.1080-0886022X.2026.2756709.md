## Summary (for evidence-reviewer; not a screening decision)

Retrospective single-center cohort, n=71 adults with biopsy-proven primary IgAN (Japan). Evaluated association between systemic immune-inflammation index (SII) and acute vs. chronic histological lesion burden. After full covariate adjustment, acute lesions showed a positive but non-significant association with SII (β=10.615, p=0.064); became significant after sensitivity analysis adjusting only for age/eGFR (p=0.043). ROC AUC 0.725 for identifying severe active lesions (>10% acute lesions), exploratory SII cutoff 618.8 (70.0% sens, 67.2% spec). Authors explicitly caveat that clinical utility "requires validation in larger cohorts." Small sample size (n=71), single-center, borderline/inconsistent statistical significance across models — flagged for evidence-reviewer's quality appraisal.
