- Notes: MAJOR FINDING for the IgAV diagnostics/clinical-phenotype sub-topic — the largest and most methodologically rigorous (centralized 3-pathologist scoring) study found on whether the IgAN-derived MEST-C/Oxford classification applies to IgAV nephritis. Key finding: only E1 (endocapillary hypercellularity) among MEST-C components was independently associated with a high-risk eGFR-decline trajectory class; fibrous crescents (not part of the pediatric ISKDC classification) were also prognostically relevant. This nuances (does not simply validate) the direct IgAN-to-IgAV extrapolation of MEST-C — relevant to research-question.md's requirement to flag such extrapolations as indirect evidence. Retrospective design, no randomization — standard limitations for an observational cohort of this kind.
