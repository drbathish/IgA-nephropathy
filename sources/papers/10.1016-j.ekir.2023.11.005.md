Key finding: complement proteins from the alternative and lectin pathways (FB, FH, MASP1/3, MASP2, C5a, C5b9) were specifically associated with the crescent (C1-2) MEST-C class, supporting complement-pathway-targeted therapy relevance to more severe/active histologic lesions.
