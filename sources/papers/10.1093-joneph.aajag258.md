Note (content summary, for evidence-reviewer, not an extraction): Meta-analysis of 9 retrospective cohorts (n=1,185 kidney transplant recipients with recurrent IgAN) assessing MEST-C component prognostic value for allograft failure. Found S, T, and C lesions consistently associated with allograft loss; M and E lesions and the total MEST-C score showed inconsistent/non-independent associations in multivariable pooling. Authors conclude total MEST-C score utility requires prospective validation before routine reporting in the transplant setting. This nuances (but is in a different population from) the Barbour et al. 2024 CJASN IgAV-nephritis MEST-C validation study already on file — both studies independently question full-panel MEST-C transportability outside the original native-kidney IgAN derivation population.
