- Notes (literature-scout, not a screening decision): Real-world observational case series, n=45 consecutive Asian-American IgAN patients treated with Nefecon at a single US clinic (Chinatown Kidney Care, New York). No placebo/active comparator arm (single-arm, descriptive, with a pre-specified ≥9-month vs <9-month treatment-duration subgroup comparison functioning as an internal exposure contrast). Reports eGFR stability and ~19% average UPCR reduction overall, with greater benefit (26% UPCR reduction, eGFR preservation) in patients completing ≥9 months of therapy versus a decline in eGFR and only ~3% UPCR reduction in the <9-month group. Authors explicitly frame findings as consistent with the NefIgArd trial (already on file: Barratt 2022 Kidney Int; Lafayette 2023 Lancet) and add real-world evidence for an understudied population (Asian-American patients) and for treatment-duration/adherence effects. Industry-adjacency note: several co-authors listed without obvious academic-only affiliations in the abstract; evidence-reviewer should check full author affiliations/disclosures for any commercial (Calliditas/Nefecon-manufacturer) ties during quality/bias appraisal, as this was not resolved at the coarse-scope stage.
