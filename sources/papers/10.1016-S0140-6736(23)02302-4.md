- Study type: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled phase 3 trial (PROTECT, NCT03762850); 2-year (110-week) final double-blind results (n=404, sparsentan vs irbesartan)
- Notes: Tier-2 RCT evidence. Reports eGFR slope preservation (chronic 2-year slope difference 1.1 mL/min/1.73m2/year, p=0.037) and sustained proteinuria reduction (40% lower vs irbesartan at 110 weeks), plus a composite kidney-failure endpoint numerically favoring sparsentan (9% vs 13%) that did not reach statistical significance (RR 0.7, 95% CI 0.4-1.2). Comparator is active (irbesartan), not placebo. Funded by Travere Therapeutics (manufacturer) — sponsor-funded industry trial, note for bias appraisal.
