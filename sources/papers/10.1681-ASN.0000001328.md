## Summary (for evidence-reviewer; not a screening decision)

Conceptual/perspective review by a leading IgAN pathogenesis research group (Suzuki lab, Juntendo University) proposing that only a subset of circulating Gd-IgA1 — termed "pathogenic Gd-IgA1" — rather than the entire circulating Gd-IgA1 pool, may drive glomerular inflammation (hematuria/proteinuria) in IgAN. Reasoning is based on re-analysis of longitudinal Gd-IgA1/proteinuria changes reported in recent therapeutic trials (not named specifically in the abstract). Proposes three potential defining features of "pathogenic Gd-IgA1": mucosal origin, molecular properties favoring selective mesangial deposition, and capacity to form immune complexes/activate complement. No new primary data generated; directly relevant background/conceptual-framing material for the mechanism/pathogenesis section of the synthesis, and potentially useful for contextualizing the already-recorded RITA/APPLAUSE/ravulizumab/povetacicept trial data referenced in this project.
