- Screening reason: RESOLVED CONSISTENCY RULE (governs this record and, going forward, the Batch 4 Nishiyama et al. inconsistency the orchestrator flagged): IgA-dominant infection-related glomerulonephritis (IgA-IRGN) is treated as a recognized, mechanistically and clinically distinct diagnostic entity from primary IgA nephropathy — a transient/reactive immune-complex glomerulonephritis driven by an identified active infection (here, MRSA urinary infection in a patient with chronic perianal sepsis), with a different natural history and management paradigm (infection clearance/antibiotics ± cautious immunosuppression) from primary IgAN's chronic, Gd-IgA1-autoimmune-driven mesangial disease. This falls outside research-question.md's population definition ("biopsy-confirmed or clinically diagnosed IgA nephropathy") unless the paper's own conclusion resolves the differential in favor of confirmed primary IgAN (i.e., IgA-IRGN is ruled out). Per PubMed's own abstract, this case's differential explicitly remained UNRESOLVED between "advanced IgAN with infection-triggered flare versus IgA-IRGN" — immunosuppression was withheld precisely because of that unresolved uncertainty, and the patient's kidney function did not recover, so the paper never establishes that the patient has primary IgAN. The tier-7 "diagnostic pitfall" carve-out for case reports is reserved for atypical or difficult-to-recognize presentations of confirmed (or genuinely IgAN-spectrum-disputed — contrast the MCD-with-mesangial-IgA case, `10.1155-crin-7021133.md`, included in this same batch) IgAN/IgAV itself, not for working through an unresolved differential against a separately-named, separately-mechanism alternative diagnosis such as IgA-IRGN. This is consistent with the Batch 4 (2026-09-18) exclusion of the analogous Nishiyama et al. case (PMID 42752782) as "a recognized but distinct diagnostic entity from primary IgA nephropathy" — that exclusion is affirmed as the correct outcome under this rule, even though it was reached via a coarse-scope judgment rather than this explicit rule at the time. No correction to the Nishiyama record is needed since it was never filed as a source record; this rule should be applied by literature-scout/evidence-reviewer to any future IgA-IRGN-vs-IgAN case report: exclude by default under the population-definition/tier-7 boundary above, unless the paper's own text concludes the patient's final diagnosis is primary IgAN (an atypical presentation), in which case it would qualify for the tier-7 carve-out as a bona fide IgAN diagnostic pitfall. Excluded per research-question.md's population/scope definition (patients here do not have a confirmed diagnosis of "IgA nephropathy" — the differential is left open, and IgA-IRGN, not IgAN, is co-equally or more consistent with the resolution reached), and per the exclusion criterion "Single-patient case reports that do not illustrate a novel/emerging intervention or diagnostic pitfall [in IgAN/IgAV itself]" since the pitfall here concerns distinguishing IgAN from a separate disease category rather than illustrating IgAN/IgAV itself.
