## Summary (for evidence-reviewer; not a screening decision)

Single-patient (n=1) case report: 17-year-old kidney transplant recipient with biopsy-confirmed recurrent crescentic IgAN 2 years post-transplant, refractory to corticosteroids and targeted-release budesonide. Rescue therapy = one rituximab infusion + 4 daratumumab infusions. Outcome: proteinuria 5.21→0.06 g/g, stable allograft function through day 482, sustained peripheral B-cell depletion (CyTOF/flow cytometry), resolution of active cellular crescent on sequential biopsy (though mesangial IgA/electron-dense deposits persisted). One mild adverse event (URI). Authors explicitly caveat: "This single case cannot establish efficacy, distinguish the contributions of rituximab and daratumumab, or exclude effects of preceding and supportive treatments." Flagged per research-question.md's tier-7 guidance as low evidentiary weight (single case) but illustrating a genuinely novel combination-rescue approach for a clinically significant, treatment-refractory IgAN phenotype.
