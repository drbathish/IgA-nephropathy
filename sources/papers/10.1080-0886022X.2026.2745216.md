Study summary (for evidence-reviewer, not a substitute for full extraction): Single-center retrospective cohort comparing clinicopathological features and renal outcomes across κ-dominant (n=21), κ-λ co-deposition (n=42), and λ-dominant (n=42) light-chain deposition patterns in primary IgAN, matched 1:2:2 on age/sex/24h-proteinuria/baseline creatinine. Primary renal endpoint: sustained ≥30% rise in serum creatinine. Renal progression-free survival differed significantly across groups (log-rank p=0.0046); after adjustment, higher κ deposition ratio was independently associated with lower hazard of renal progression (HR 0.96 per 1-percentage-point increase, 95% CI 0.94–0.99, p=0.011). Median follow-up 25 months (IQR 18–36). Proposed as a possible histopathologic marker of a distinct, less-aggressive immune-complex deposition phenotype.
